Dépannage

Most problems show up in two USB commands: link log for the Minitel side and boot for the XIAO.

The Minitel shows its répertoire, or falls asleep

At power-on and after a standby the Minitel 2 is back at 1200 baud in Vidéotex 40 columns, on its local menu, the répertoire. In that state it answers no protocol query, so the firmware cannot take it back by itself.

  • Press Fnct + Sommaire to leave the répertoire. Within a few seconds the firmware finds the terminal again, sets it up and shows the welcome page.
  • Press Fnct + E, then A, to disable the automatic standby, so it does not happen again.
  • Once per outage the firmware also sends the protocol reset (1B 39 7F) at 1200 then 9600 baud and probes again; link log shows whether it was acknowledged. The Minitel 2 guide says a peripheral can command standby exit from ROM CV3/BV9, but no source found gives the codes.

Nothing is lost meanwhile: the node keeps receiving, retrying direct messages and serving the phone app.

No answer from the Minitel

  • Mode. In the Téléinformatique mode (Fnct+T, A) the protocol is off by design: no status, no speed change, and keystrokes may not reach the DIN port. Go back with Fnct+T, V.
  • Speed. The firmware probes 9600, 1200, 4800 and 300 baud every 3 s. link log lists each round without an answer. If no speed ever answers, check the wiring before anything else.
  • Wiring. D6 must go to the transistor's emitter, D7 through 9.1 kΩ to the 220 kΩ / DIN 3 node, the interface on 3V3, DIN 2 to GND. With the bring-up sketch (firmware/minitel-test/), sense tells whether D7 sees the Minitel's TX (pulled down by the pin's own pull-down: LOW) or its RX (held HIGH by the Minitel), which catches swapped wires.
  • Pin numbering. Check pin 5 with a multimeter: 8.5–15 V against pin 2 with the Minitel on. If you find it elsewhere, your view of the plug is mirrored.
  • By hand. link try 1B 39 74 sends the speed query at the current speed and prints the reply.

Parity errors, noise on D7

The Minitel's TX is an open collector with only the 220 kΩ pull-up behind it, and the CRT is next to it: D7 idles with hundreds of very short glitches. The UART filters them at 1200 baud; at 9600 some get through as bytes with bad parity, counted by link log (parity_err). Bytes with bad parity are dropped, and a burst of them is taken as a sign the terminal was reset.

  • Lower the pull-up R3 from 220 kΩ to about 10 kΩ.
  • Keep the DIN cable short and the ground wire solid.

Crashes and restarts

  • boot tells why the XIAO last restarted (power-on, reset button, watchdog, lockup, software reset, which includes crashes) and prints the last crash record: pc, lr, the fault registers, the task and the uptime. The État page (Réglages 11) shows it too.
  • Resolve it with the ELF of the build that crashed: examples/companion_radio/ui-minitel/tools/crash2line.sh <pc> <lr> firmware-<commit>.elf.
  • stack shows how much of the 4 KB loop stack was never used; a small number means the stack came close to overflowing.
  • boot clear forgets the record once dealt with.

Direct messages never arrive

A direct message is encrypted for a pair of keys. If the sender is not in your contacts, the node cannot decrypt its messages: they are dropped without a trace, and you cannot write to it either.

  • Ask the other side to send an advert, or add the node yourself: Contacts, N, its 64-digit public key, the type and a name.
  • Check the auto-add settings (Réglages 7): with auto-add off for that type, adverts do not create contacts.
  • Give your own key to correspondents from Réglages 13 (Mon identité).
  • A ? in the hops column means no known path: messages flood. Forget the path on the contact sheet forces a new one to be learnt.

Other things

  • Cannot send: "heure non réglée". Set the clock (Sommaire 6), let the app set it, or enable "envoyer sans heure".
  • Accents look wrong in 80 columns. The Mixte French set only has à é è ù ç ° £ §; other accented letters are sent correctly but shown bare.
  • Upload fails. The 1200-baud touch sometimes misses: retry, or double-tap the XIAO's reset button and upload to the bootloader's port.
  • The identity changed after a flash. The file system was erased. Restore the key with set prv.key and reboot (see identity backup).
